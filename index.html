Good questions.
Clear answers.

What Talay handles, what stays with your team, and how you pay.

Does it reply under our centre’s name?

Yes. Talay uses your centre’s name, prices and course information. The diver is speaking to your centre, not browsing a marketplace. Your team confirms the booking.

Does Talay bring us new leads?

Talay works on inquiries your centre already receives. It answers, asks qualifying questions and follows up when a conversation goes quiet. It does not bring new traffic or replace your marketing.

What if we’re fully booked?

Talay never promises a place, holds a spot or confirms availability on its own. It collects the diver’s dates, certification level and group size, then routes the booking request to your team.

What happens with medical questions or complaints?

They go to your team instead of receiving an automated answer. Talay does not give medical advice or decide whether someone is fit to dive. Your team handles the conversation.

Will it keep chasing people who don’t reply?

No. When a lead goes quiet, Talay sends two follow-ups over three days, then stops. The purpose is to pick up an unfinished conversation, not keep messaging indefinitely.

Can it reply in the diver’s language?

Yes. Talay replies in the language the diver uses, with your centre’s prices and course information. It asks for the details your team needs: dates, certification level and group size.

What do we pay, and when?

There is no setup cost, monthly fee or contract. Talay earns a commission on confirmed bookings through the system, taken from the diver’s deposit. The rate is 15% for single fun dives and Discover Scuba Diving, and 12% for courses and fun dive packages. Deposit amounts and cancellation terms are agreed with you before setup.

Do we need to replace our booking software?

No. Talay handles inquiries and follow-ups; it does not run your operations software. To get started, we need your prices, course details and the rules for handing conversations to your team. Message us and we’ll show you the flow.
