What Mia does

Talay is set up with the centre's own details: courses, prices, schedules, meeting point and rules. Mia only picks the right answer; the texts are the centre's own.

Answers in seconds, 24/7

More than 160 standard diver questions, under the centre's name, in the diver's own language.

Turns a chat into a booking

A link where the diver picks a date and pays a deposit by card. The team gets the booking by email.

Follows up

When a diver goes quiet on WhatsApp, Mia sends a friendly reminder, at most three times, never at night.

Knows when to step back

Medical questions, groups, schedule changes: the team gets an alert, and Mia stays quiet as soon as a person replies.
