What happens next

From this chat to Mia answering for your own centre. You decide at every step.

  1. 1 Today

    Try Mia

    Test her on the demo number, alone or with your team. Ask the questions your divers ask you.

  2. 2 About 20 minutes

    Tell us about your centre

    A short form with your courses, prices, times and rules. It saves as you go, so you can stop and continue later.

    Open the form
  3. 3 1 to 2 days

    We set up your assistant

    With your own prices and texts, under your centre's name. Nothing changes in your system.

  4. 4 As long as you like

    You and your team test it

    First on a test number, not with real divers. We adjust it until you are happy.

  5. 5 When you say go

    Mia answers your divers

    On your own number, all day or only outside office hours. You follow everything in your zone and can switch it off at any moment.
